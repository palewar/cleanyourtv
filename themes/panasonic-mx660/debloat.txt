# Panasonic TH-65MX660DX 65" Google TV (MOKA R4_GTV, Realtek rtd2851m, 2 GB), Android TV 14
# Cleaned on 2026-10-26. Personal list, kept in work/ (git-ignored).
#
# Check after a firmware update (ADB open again first):
#   TV_HOST=192.168.1.24 tools/venv/bin/python -I tools/check.py work/panasonic-th65mx660dx/debloat.txt com.google.android.apps.tv.launcherx
#   ... add --fix to disable what came back and put the launcher back.
# Undo everything: TV_HOST=192.168.1.24 tools/restore.sh work/panasonic-th65mx660dx/debloat.txt
#   (or backup/panasonic-th65mx660dx-20261026/restore.sh)
# Everything is DISABLED, not uninstalled.
#
# MOKA firmware: com.android.tv.settings re-enables a fixed list of packages at every cold boot (power cut or
# unplug; standby does not). Lines marked [boot] come back then; check.py reports them as drift, and --fix only
# keeps them off until the next cold boot. See docs/devices/acer-r4g.md.
#
# Home: launcherx and setupwraith stay ENABLED on this firmware (disabling them gives a black Home after a cold
# boot). The new launcher takes Home through HomeKeyService with stock_home_redirect=launcherx, which is why
# check.py gets launcherx as the second argument.
#
# Firmware updaters are disabled by the owner's choice; they come back at every cold boot anyway ([boot]).
# Kept on purpose: Play Store, Play Services, TV Settings, Live TV (com.realtek.tv) and the HDMI inputs,
# remote/Bluetooth, keyboard, the streaming apps, YouTube Music, Apple TV.
